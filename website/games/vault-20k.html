How it plays

Tumble

Winning symbols burst and leave the board. The reels above fall into place as whole stacks, and new symbols drop in until no new win appears.

Multiplier strike

Lightning turns a landed symbol into a multiplier from 2x to 1000x. In the base game, when a tumble sequence ends with a win, its multipliers are added together and multiply that win.

Free Spins & the bank

4+ scatters award 15 free spins. Multipliers that land in a winning sequence add to a total that grows all feature long. Any winning sequence that lands another multiplier is paid at the whole total.

Buy Feature

For 100x the bet, Free Spins start right away. Operators can switch it off for markets that don't allow bonus buys.

Ante Bet

Playing 1.25x the bet triggers Free Spins about 1.36x as often. Buy Feature is unavailable while Ante is on.

Built for phones

Runs at 60fps without stutter on mid-range devices, with 44px touch targets, no screen shake, and a layout that never jumps during play.
