# 02 Legal Compliance (Georgia First, Global Next)

## Goal
Define legal feasibility, licensing path, and compliance requirements before commercial distribution.

## Important

No provider of quality will onboard uncertified, non-compliant game content.

## Tasks

1. Georgia market mapping:
   - Identify regulator/licensing pathways relevant to online slots
   - Identify permitted RTP ranges and disclosure obligations
   - Identify player protection obligations
2. Define expansion model:
   - Market tiering:
     - Tier A: high-value jurisdictions
     - Tier B: lighter entry jurisdictions
   - Compliance delta per market
3. Create legal requirement matrix:
   - Required certifications per jurisdiction
   - Data privacy requirements
   - Responsible gambling requirements
   - AML/KYC touchpoints (if operator-facing stack later)
4. Define prohibited patterns:
   - No hidden RTP manipulation
   - No misleading “near miss” abuse outside regulated allowance
5. Draft legal artifacts list:
   - Game rules disclosures
   - RTP disclosure format
   - Terms and fairness statements

## Deliverables

- `docs/compliance-matrix.md`
- `docs/georgia-licensing-notes.md`
- `docs/global-expansion-matrix.md`

## Exit Criteria

- Georgia compliance path documented.
- Certification/lab needs known.
- Launch blocking legal unknowns reduced to zero or tracked decisions.
