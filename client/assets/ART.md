# Vault 20K: art

## Colchis Treasury (October 2026)

Every image in the Colchis set was generated by the game's owner with Google Gemini from
prompts written for this game (a style bible, one approved style anchor image attached to
every prompt), then reviewed against the bible and cut out. No image was traced or copied
from another game, provider or brand, and no prompt named one.

| Client file (`symbols/`) | What it is | Master (`art-src/colchis/`) |
|---|---|---|
| `Crown.webp` | The Diadem (top symbol, the style anchor) | `crown.webp` |
| `HourGlass.webp` | The Sand-glass | `hourglass.webp` |
| `Ring.webp` | The Signet Ring (a seven-armed Borjgali on the seal) | `ring.webp` |
| `Chaile.webp` | The Ritual Cup | `cup.webp` |

Effects (`client/assets/fx/`):

| Client file | What it is | Master |
|---|---|---|
| `splash.webp`, `shards`, `glint`, `swirl`, `flare` | The board effects, frame by frame (one row of 192 px cells) | `fx_*.webp` (generated sheets, cut on their grid) |
| `coin.webp` | The scatter turning like a coin (8 cells of 256 px, alpha; cell 1 = Scatter.webp) | `coin_turn_22` / `_67` (the owner's Gemini turns), `_90` (edge from the first turn sheet), `_00` (face-on: the scatter's near half mirrored) + `scatter` |
| `beam.webp` | The sunbeam of the multiplier reveal (stretched along its length) | `fx_beam.webp` |

Not used yet: `drop`, `coin_face`, `coin_edge`, `logo`. The big-win coin shower uses `coin.webp`. The effect
strips are light on black turned into light with alpha (alpha = brightness, colour un-blended),
so they add up correctly over the transparent board.

Cutting: objects rendered on black were cut with a flood from the image border; the body is
opaque and the soft bloom around it is un-blended from black (alpha = brightness), so a glow
stays a glow on any backdrop. The gems and the corner block take the convex hull of their
bright rim as the body. Frame sheets were cut on their grid, flickering frames dropped, and
the cell edges faded to black. `node tools/build-vault-art.mjs` sizes and encodes everything.

The older art (`FREESPINS_INFO-removebg-preview.webp`) is unchanged.
