# Audio sources and licences

Every sound in `sfx/` and `music/` is built by `tools/build-audio.js` from the
originals in `art-src/audio/` (trimmed, layered, levelled and re-encoded). Every
original is **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**: free for commercial use, no
attribution required. Credit is given here anyway, as a courtesy. No sound is
taken from, or made to imitate, any other slot game or provider.

Since 2026-10-11 (palette v2) every sound effect is built from the Freesound
picks below (chosen per action on the audition page, `npm run audition`; the
choices live in `art-src/audio/selection.json`). The Kenney and OpenGameArt
originals further down are kept in `art-src/audio/` but no longer used for
sound effects. Since 2026-10-26 the three music moods are the owner's own tracks made
with Google Lyria 3 (see the section below); they are not CC0 recordings.
