    Game Info

    Game Rules & How to Play

    Tumble Feature

    After every win, the winning symbols are removed. The symbols above fall down to fill the gaps, and new symbols drop in from the top.

    • If the new board has another win, it tumbles again. This repeats until no new win appears.
    • All wins in one tumble sequence are added together.
    • Multiplier symbols can land on any spin or tumble. They only count when the sequence ends with a win. See Game Rules for how they apply in the base game and in Free Spins.

    Free Spins & Max Win

    4 or more Scatter symbols on the grid at the same time, including after a tumble, award 15 Free Spins.

    • During Free Spins, 3 or more Scatter symbols award 5 more Free Spins.
    • Free Spins are played at the bet of the spin that triggered them and cost nothing.
    • Free Spins play automatically once you continue from the feature screen. They cannot be stopped, but a press of Spin speeds up the current spin.
    • Multiplier symbols from 2x to 1000x can land. In Free Spins they add up to a total multiplier for the whole round.

    The maximum win is 20,000x the bet.

    • A single base game spin pays at most 20,000x the bet.
    • In Free Spins, the total win of the round, including the spin that triggered it, is capped at 20,000x the bet.
    • When the cap is reached, the round ends at once and any remaining Free Spins are lost. If the triggering spin already reaches the cap, no Free Spins are played.

    Special Bets & Buy Feature

    Ante Bet raises the cost of each spin to 1.25x the bet and increases the chance of triggering Free Spins.

    • Wins are still paid on the bet, not on the higher spin cost.
    • Free Spins are never charged, with or without Ante Bet.
    • Ante Bet can only be switched between rounds.

    Buy Feature plays a spin for 100x the bet that is guaranteed to land at least 4 Scatter symbols and award 15 Free Spins.

    • The cost is shown in a confirmation window before you buy.
    • Buy Feature is not available while Ante Bet is on.
    • Ante Bet and Buy Feature may not be available in every market.

    Bet Menu

    • Use − and + beside the Spin button to lower or raise the bet one step at a time.
    • The current bet is shown under Bet.
    • The bet cannot be changed while a round is in play, including Free Spins.
    • During free rounds given by the casino, the bet is fixed by that offer.
    • All pays in the Paytable are shown at the current bet.

    Autoplay Rules

    Press the Autoplay button and choose 10, 25, 50 or ∞ (until stopped) spins. The spins left are shown on the button.

    Autoplay stops when:

    • you press Autoplay or Spin again (the current spin still finishes);
    • the chosen number of spins has been played;
    • a spin cannot be played, for example when the balance is too low;
    • the casino pauses the game or a session reminder is shown;
    • a stop condition you chose is met: your loss in this run reaches the limit, one spin wins at least the amount you set (Free Spins count as part of the spin that won them), or Free Spins are won (after they are played).

    If Free Spins are won during Autoplay, they are played first. Autoplay then continues.

    Autoplay may not be available, or may be limited to fewer spins, in some markets.

    Main Interface Guide

    • Spin: starts a spin. While a spin is in play, press it to quick stop (where the market allows it). Hold it, or the space bar, to switch on Turbo and start Autoplay.
    • − / +: change the bet. Tap Bet to pick from every bet.
    • Autoplay: plays a chosen number of spins automatically, with stop conditions.
    • Speed: normal, quick or turbo. Speed only changes how fast a spin is shown, never its result.
    • Buy Feature and Ante Bet: beside the board.
    • Menu: sound, music, effects, speed, left-hand mode, reduced motion, battery saver, vibration and full screen, where your device supports them.
    • Info: these rules and the paytable.
    • Balance, Bet, Win: your balance, the current bet and this round's win.
    • The message line shows tips, the current win and game notices.

    A malfunction voids all pays and plays.
